One file in, everything out

Change the patient in src/data.ts — a value, a reference range, a date — and all four templates re-render with new flags, new bar positions, and a new story. Statuses are computed with value < refLow → low logic in code, the same way a real clinic system would derive them. Two of the eight sample results sit just outside range (vitamin D, HbA1c), and the doctor's note and care plan are built around exactly those two.

Marigold Clinic is fictional. Sample data — not medical advice. All names and results in this template are invented.
